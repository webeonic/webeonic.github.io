Darwin

A self-hosted personal assistant. Single user: its owner.

What it is

Darwin is a private assistant that runs on hardware its owner controls. It is not a product, has no sign-up, and is not offered to anyone else. It reads its owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar so it can answer questions about them and produce a short daily briefing.

What it does with Google data

  • Reads recent mail and calendar events for the signed-in account.
  • Summarises them into a daily digest delivered privately to the owner.
  • Sends or labels mail only when the owner explicitly asks it to.

It does not read anyone else's mailbox, and there are no other users to share anything with.

Privacy

The privacy policy describes exactly which data is accessed, where it is processed, and how to revoke access at any time.
